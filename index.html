Privacy Policy for Quick Tick

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Quick Tick is made by AjE. Contact: quicktick.help@gmail.com

This policy explains what Quick Tick ("the app," "we," "us") does with your information. In short: what you track stays on your phone. The app contacts Google's servers only to check a Quick Tick Pro subscription, to send a crash report if the app crashes, to send anonymous usage statistics if you agree, and, if you connect Google Drive, to back up to your own Drive.

Your tracking data stays on your phone

Your categories, logs, notes, goals, limits, costs, streaks, milestones, countdowns and settings are stored only on your device. We don't have a server that receives them, and we can't see them. Quick Tick does not require an account or login.

Back up to file

In Settings, Back up to file saves a copy of your Quick Tick data to a file in a place you pick, for example your phone's storage, Google Drive or OneDrive. We never receive this file. Once it's saved, it's handled by the service you chose, under that service's own privacy policy. Restore from file reads a backup file you pick back into the app on your device.

Google Drive backup (optional, Quick Tick Pro)

You can connect your Google account so Quick Tick backs up to your own Google Drive, once a day or when you tap Back up now. A backup contains your categories, logs, notes, durations, reminders and app settings, the same as Back up to file. It is stored in a private app folder in your Google Drive that only Quick Tick can read. It goes straight from your phone to Google; it never passes through our servers and we cannot see it.

Quick Tick asks Google for two things only: access to that app folder, and your email address so the app can show which account is connected. Your email address stays on your phone.

You can stop at any time in Settings ▸ Disconnect Google Drive, and choose to delete your backups from Google Drive when you do. You can also remove Quick Tick's access in your Google account's security settings.

Google user data

This section covers the data Quick Tick receives from Google when you connect Google Drive.

  • What Quick Tick can access. Two permissions only. First, Quick Tick's own private app folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission). It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive. Second, your Google account's email address.
  • How it is used. The app folder is used only to save your Quick Tick backups, list them, restore one when you ask, and delete old ones. The app keeps the 7 most recent. Your email address is used only to show which account is connected.
  • Where it is stored. Backups are stored only in your own Google Drive, and your email address only on your phone. We never read, receive or store your Google data on our servers.
  • Sharing. We do not share, transfer or sell your Google data to anyone. It is not used for advertising, and no person reads it.
  • Deleting it. Settings ▸ Disconnect Google Drive removes Quick Tick's access, forgets the email address and can delete your backups from Drive. Uninstalling the app also removes the email address from your phone. You can also remove Quick Tick's access in your Google account's security settings.

Quick Tick's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Android backup

If Android's built-in backup is turned on for your phone, Android may back up Quick Tick's data to your Google account, under Google's privacy policy. We don't receive or have access to that backup. You can turn Android backup off in your phone's settings.

CSV and PDF export

Quick Tick Pro can export your history as a CSV file or as a PDF. The file is saved on your device, or wherever you pick when saving, and is not sent to us. It only leaves your device if you choose to share it, for example to Google Drive or by email. It then goes only to the app or service you pick, under that app's own privacy policy.

Quick Tick Pro purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details (card numbers, billing address and so on).

To confirm that a Pro subscription is valid, the app sends the purchase's details (a purchase token and product ID) to our verification server, which runs on Google Firebase. For this check only:

  • The app signs in to Firebase anonymously. This creates a random sign-in ID that is not linked to your name, email or Google account.
  • Firebase's tools also send a Firebase installation ID with each request to our server.
  • Google Play Integrity / Firebase App Check is used to confirm that the request comes from the real Quick Tick app.

We store the purchase token, the anonymous sign-in ID and whether your subscription is active, only to keep Pro working correctly and to prevent fraud. This information is not linked to your tracking data, is not used for anything else, and is not sold or shared. As with any internet connection, Google Firebase sees your device's IP address when the app contacts our server. We don't use it to work out where you are.

Crash reports

If Quick Tick crashes, it sends a crash report to Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the problem. A report contains technical details of the crash, your phone model, Android version, app version, free memory and storage, and a random installation ID. It does not contain your categories, logs, notes or costs. Crash reports are kept for 90 days.

Usage statistics (optional)

After you've used Quick Tick for a few days, it asks once whether you want to help improve the app. If you agree, it sends anonymous usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase: that the app was opened, that a category was created and its type (tracking, milestone or countdown), that a log was added and where from (app, widget, notification, tile or reminder), that the upgrade screen was shown and for which feature, that a purchase was started or completed, and that a Pro feature was used. Google also records general device details (phone model, Android version, app version, language and country) and when each event happened.

These statistics never include your category names, notes, costs or the times of your logs, and are not linked to your name, email or advertising ID. If you don't agree, or haven't answered yet, none of this is sent. You can change your choice any time in Settings ▸ Help improve Quick Tick.

App lock and reminders

App lock (Quick Tick Pro) uses your phone's own fingerprint, face or screen-lock check. Quick Tick never sees or stores your fingerprint, face data or PIN; it only remembers that app lock is on. Reminders (Quick Tick Pro) are scheduled and shown by your phone. Their times and settings are stored only on your device and are not sent to us.

Ratings

Quick Tick may occasionally ask you to rate it, using Google Play's own rating window. Your rating and review go to Google Play under Google's privacy policy; we don't receive anything from that window.

No ads

Quick Tick does not show ads and does not use advertising trackers or the advertising ID. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or messages.

Deleting your data

On your phone. Settings ▸ Erase all data deletes everything Quick Tick has stored on your device: your categories, logs, notes, reminders and settings. Uninstalling the app does the same. Backup and export files you saved elsewhere, including backups in your own Google Drive, are yours to delete; Settings ▸ Disconnect Google Drive can delete the Drive backups for you.

On our servers, on request. Email quicktick.help@gmail.com and include the Google Play order number from your purchase receipt (it starts with "GPA.") so we can find your record. We will then delete:

  • your purchase and subscription record (the purchase token, product ID and whether Pro is active), and
  • the anonymous Firebase sign-in ID linked to it.

We do this within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Crash reports and usage statistics are stored under a random app-installation ID, not under your name, email or purchase, so we can't pick out yours from an email request. They are deleted automatically instead:

  • crash reports after 90 days (Firebase Crashlytics' retention), and
  • usage statistics, which are only collected if you agreed to them, after the data retention period set in Google Analytics for Firebase, at most 14 months. Google may keep summary reports that don't identify anyone.

To stop usage statistics at any time, turn off Settings ▸ Help improve Quick Tick. Uninstalling the app also stops crash reports and resets the app-installation ID.

What Google Play keeps. Your order history and payment records are kept by Google Play under Google's own privacy policy. We can't delete them; you can see them in your Google account's Play payments and subscriptions.

Children's privacy

Quick Tick is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
