Privacy Policy for Quick Tick Last updated: [9/15/2026] This Privacy Policy describes how Quick Tick ("the App," "we," "us") handles your information. Summary Quick Tick is a habit and event-tracking app. Your logged activity data is stored on your device and is not collected, viewed, or shared by us. We do not require an account or login to use the App. Information We Collect Data You Enter Category names you create (e.g., custom labels for what you're tracking) Timestamps of logged events Cost values you optionally assign to categories, and your running cost totals This data is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to it, and it is not transmitted to us or any third party by the App itself. Purchase Information If you subscribe to Quick Tick Pro, your purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your payment details (card numbers, billing address, etc.) — Google handles all payment processing according to Google Play's own privacy policy. To verify an active subscription, the App communicates your purchase/subscription status (not your personal payment details) with a verification service we operate, which checks your entitlement against Google Play on our behalf. This service receives only subscription status information, not your logged activity data. Automatically Collected Information The App does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking libraries. We do not collect device identifiers, location data, or usage analytics. How We Use Information Since your logged activity data stays on your device, we do not use it for any purpose — we simply don't have access to it. Purchase/subscription status is used solely to determine whether Pro features are unlocked on your device. Data Sharing We do not sell, rent, or share your logged activity data with any third party, because we do not collect or have access to it in the first place. Subscription and billing information is shared only with Google Play as necessary to process your purchase and verify your subscription — see Google Play's Privacy Policy for how Google handles that information. Data Storage and Deletion Your logged data lives in the App's local storage on your device. Uninstalling the App removes this data from your device. Children's Privacy Quick Tick is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date.
