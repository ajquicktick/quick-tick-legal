Privacy Policy for Quick Tick

Effective date: October 11, 2026

Quick Tick is made by AjE. Contact: quicktick.help@gmail.com

This policy explains what Quick Tick ("the app," "we," "us") does with your information. In short: what you track stays on your phone. The app only contacts our server to check a Quick Tick Pro subscription.

Your tracking data stays on your phone

Your categories, logs, notes, goals, limits, costs, streaks, milestones, countdowns and settings are stored only on your device. We don't have a server that receives them, and we can't see them. Quick Tick does not require an account or login.

Back up to file

In Settings, Back up to file saves a copy of your Quick Tick data to a file in a place you pick, for example your phone's storage, Google Drive or OneDrive. We never receive this file. Once it's saved, it's handled by the service you chose, under that service's own privacy policy. Restore from file reads a backup file you pick back into the app on your device.

Android backup

If Android's built-in backup is turned on for your phone, Android may back up Quick Tick's data to your Google account, under Google's privacy policy. We don't receive or have access to that backup. You can turn Android backup off in your phone's settings.

CSV and PDF export

Quick Tick Pro can export your history as a CSV file or as a PDF. The file is saved on your device, or wherever you pick when saving, and is not sent to us. It only leaves your device if you choose to share it, for example to Google Drive or by email. It then goes only to the app or service you pick, under that app's own privacy policy.

Quick Tick Pro purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details (card numbers, billing address and so on).

To confirm that a Pro subscription is valid, the app sends the purchase's details (a purchase token and product ID) to our verification server, which runs on Google Firebase. For this check only:

  • The app signs in to Firebase anonymously. This creates a random sign-in ID that is not linked to your name, email or Google account.
  • Firebase's tools also send a Firebase installation ID with each request to our server.
  • Google Play Integrity / Firebase App Check is used to confirm that the request comes from the real Quick Tick app.

We store the purchase token, the anonymous sign-in ID and whether your subscription is active, only to keep Pro working correctly and to prevent fraud. This information is not linked to your tracking data, is not used for anything else, and is not sold or shared. As with any internet connection, Google Firebase sees your device's IP address when the app contacts our server. We don't use it to work out where you are.

If you don't buy Pro, the app does not contact our server.

No ads, analytics or tracking

Quick Tick does not show ads and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, messages or usage analytics.

Deleting your data

Settings ▸ Erase all data deletes everything Quick Tick has stored on your device. Uninstalling the app does the same. Backup and export files you saved elsewhere are yours to delete. To have your purchase record removed from our server, email us at quicktick.help@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

Quick Tick is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here and update the effective date above.
