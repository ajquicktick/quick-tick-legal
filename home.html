What Quick Tick does

Quick Tick is an Android app for keeping count of anything: coffee, workouts, phone pickups, glasses of water. Tap once and it's logged. Use it for habits you're building, habits you're breaking, or anything you're simply curious about. Milestones count the time since you last did something you're cutting out, and countdowns count the days to something coming up.

  • Log from a home screen widget, the live notification or a Quick Settings tile, without opening the app
  • Daily or weekly limits, streaks, costs per log, notes and a full history
  • Quick Tick Pro adds insights and charts, reminders, app lock, CSV and PDF export, and automatic backup to your own Google Drive

Your data

What you track stays on your phone. Quick Tick has no account or login, and we have no server that receives your categories, logs or notes. If you turn on Google Drive backup, your backups go straight from your phone to a private app folder in your own Google Drive. Read the full privacy policy.
